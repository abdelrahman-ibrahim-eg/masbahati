# Sources and licenses

Shown in the app under the menu entry "عن التطبيق · المصادر".

- Quran text: Tanzil Quran Text (Uthmani, v1.1), https://tanzil.net, CC BY 3.0, used verbatim and unchanged. See TANZIL-LICENSE.txt.
- Quran font: Amiri Quran, SIL Open Font License. See OFL-AmiriQuran.txt.
- Recitation audio and ayah timings: https://www.mp3quran.net (public API and audio files, streamed by the reader, not stored in this repo apart from derived timing offsets in timings.json).
- Morning and evening adhkar text: Hisn al-Muslim by Sheikh Said bin Ali bin Wahf al-Qahtani, via OpenDua v0.0.4 (https://opendua.org), CC BY 4.0, prepared by Ihsan Labs, a Human Development Fund initiative. Data licence: https://opendua.org/licence/data
- Source (takhrij), virtue (fadl), hadith text and vocabulary notes for each dhikr: Morning-And-Evening-Adhkar-DB by Seen Arabic, https://github.com/Seen-Arabic/Morning-And-Evening-Adhkar-DB, MIT License (below). It is based on Hisn al-Muslim and its commentary by al-Qahtani and two books by Sheikh Walid al-Muqaddam. The hadith gradings are quoted as written in that source.

Human review: the texts need review by a qualified person. Check them against a trusted printed copy before relying on them.
